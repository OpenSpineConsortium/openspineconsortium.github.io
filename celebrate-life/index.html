Celebrate Life

A soccer night for kids in treatment for cancer and blood disorders. On October 24 the entire second field is reserved for a game played only by pediatric hematology and oncology patients, coached by professional soccer players from Southeast Michigan, while the Detroit City FC Halloween Trunk or Treat runs alongside.

  • WhenSaturday, October 24 · kickoff 6:45 PM
  • WhereField 2, reserved for you
    3401 E Lafayette St, Detroit, MI 48207
  • Who playsEvery pediatric hematology and oncology patient under 18. Families welcome on the sideline.
  • Who coachesProfessional soccer players from Southeast Michigan, on the field with the kids for the whole game.
  • CostFree.
