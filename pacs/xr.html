What this needs from the film

LL and SS need only the L1 and S1 superior endplates. PI and PT additionally need the femoral head centres — visible on a standing full-spine lateral, usually not on a coned lumbar lateral. On a film without femoral heads those two are reported as n/a rather than estimated.
