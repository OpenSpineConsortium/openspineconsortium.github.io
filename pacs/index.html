What this needs from the film

This is a DRR — the CT of the case in the other tab, integrated along the patient's true bicoxofemoral axis, so the projection is the one the angles are actually defined in. Constructions come from ostk's own 2-D projection (ostk.drr + ostk.project2d), and the values are copied verbatim from the CT bundle — the two tabs report the same numbers by construction.

On a real film, LL and SS need only the L1 and S1 endplates, but PI and PT need the femoral head centres — absent from a coned lumbar lateral, in which case ostk returns them as n/a rather than estimating.

Research & educational use only. A research demonstration — not a medical device and not for clinical or diagnostic use. Measurements are illustrative and must not guide patient care.
