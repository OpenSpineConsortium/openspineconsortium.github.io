Beethoven

Ask for access to Beethoven

Beethoven is for Wayne State students and staff working with the lab. Fill this in and the lab lead will look at it. Once you are approved, Beethoven starts working the next time you click its icon on your portal.

What happens next

The lead approves requests by hand, usually within a few days. You can come back to this page and send the same request again to see where it stands: it will say pending, approved, or not approved. When you are approved, click the Beethoven icon on your portal; you do not need to wait for an e-mail.

The same approval covers Beethoven for VS Code: once you are approved, its setup wizard lets you through at the Wayne State sign-in step.
