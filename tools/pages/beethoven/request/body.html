Beethoven

Ask for access to Beethoven

Beethoven is for Wayne State students and staff working with the lab. Fill this in and the lab lead will look at it. Once you are approved, an invitation to Beethoven's private Chrome listing comes to the Google account you give here.

What happens next

  1. If the service e-mails your Wayne State address a link, open it and confirm the request is yours. That link is how the lab knows the request came from your mailbox.
  2. The lab lead reads the request, usually within a few days, and approves it for Beethoven, approves you as a lab member only, or says no.
  3. Approved for Beethoven: an invitation to its private listing in the Chrome Web Store comes to the Google account you gave. Open it in Chrome, signed in to that account, and install Beethoven there. The listing opens only for invited accounts; there is no other place to get it.
  4. Open your grid portal and click the Beethoven icon.

Beethoven works on one computer per approved account, and only for the AccessID that was approved. A second computer, a second Chrome profile or a reinstall counts as a new installation and is refused until the lab lead moves your approval to it. Sharing a copy or an account breaks the agreement.

You can send the same request again to see where it stands: the page says it was sent and is waiting for the lab lead, that you are already approved, or that it was not approved.

An approval for Beethoven also covers Beethoven for VS Code: its setup wizard lets you through at the Wayne State sign-in step.
