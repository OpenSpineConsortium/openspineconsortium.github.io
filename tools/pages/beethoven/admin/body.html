Access admin

Requests, approvals and installations of Beethoven, both editions, and who reads the lab’s storage. For the maintainer only.

The value in ~/.beethoven-vendor/admin_token. Kept in this browser tab only, until you close it.

One installation per account: Checking Off lets every approved account work from any number of installations, with nobody refused as a second installation and no seat bound or moved; it is meant for a short while, such as while people replace an old copy, and the seats bound before hold again once it is back on.

Waiting for a decision

Every request the form stored. When the service e-mails students, a request is marked Mailbox not confirmed until the student confirms from their Wayne State mailbox; it lapses after seven days if they do not, and they can send the form again.

Approved

After an approval for Beethoven: invited once the Google account is in the Google Group Beethoven-users, which opens the private Chrome listing to it, then installed once the first installation has checked in and holds the seat.

Lab storage, read only

People who asked to read named folders of the lab’s storage on the grid (/rs/rs_grp_oschome), or all of it, apart from Beethoven. A reader gets the lab group’s read on their folders and nothing more: never write, never another folder. All of the lab storage is everything in it that the group may read and the volume’s owner owns; a folder another person owns stays as they set it. The grid tool on your PC applies the list at its next run; the Unix group stays C&IT’s. The person is not e-mailed: tell them yourself.
