Surgical hardware

11 of the 802 records carry surgical instrumentation, in four kinds. Every implant is labelled as its own structure rather than left inside the bone that a segmenter had absorbed it into — each one below is rendered from the released segmentation.

Every case was read by a radiologist before it was labelled. Filter on hardware_labelled in the manifest before any measurement that depends on a gap between bones: a cage-bridged interspace reads as “no gap” exactly as a congenitally fused transitional vertebra does. In 9 cases the femoral head itself is an implant, which is the landmark pelvic incidence and pelvic tilt are measured from.
